# Module 06: Practice Tracks

This module is the laboratory of the curriculum. Upskilling occurs when you struggle with real problems, write code, run it, and critically evaluate the results. You will complete 10 reading katas, 10 progressively challenging mock ticket modifications, 5 progressive build projects, and 8-12 Socratic design refactoring exercises.

## Document Index

1. **[01-reading-katas.md](01-reading-katas.md):** 10 code-anchored Socratic reading exercises with detailed evaluation rubrics.
2. **[02-modify-tickets.md](02-modify-tickets.md):** 10 progressive ticketing exercises, from Easy to Expert, covering different surfaces of the system.
3. **[03-build-projects.md](03-build-projects.md):** 5 progressive architectural projects to design and build.
4. **[04-senior-katas.md](04-senior-katas.md):** 10 open-ended design and refactoring exercises with no single correct answer.
